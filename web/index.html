NASA SPLICE · Safe & Precise Landing Workflow

NASA STMD SPLICE ↗

Why a 500 m micro-relocation works in practice: NASA’s Safe and Precise Landing – Integrated Capabilities Evolution (SPLICE) suite autonomously maps surface hazards during descent and diverts the lander away from shadows, craters, and steep slopes.

01 · ORBIT → 10 KM TRN · Terrain Relative Navigation Onboard camera matches live descent photos against preloaded LROC & LOLA orbital maps so the Descent & Landing Computer (DLC) locks global coordinates.
02 · 10 KM → 500 M NDL · Navigation Doppler Lidar Four fiber-coupled laser telescopes measure line-of-sight velocity and altitude with centimeter-per-second precision, replacing legacy radar altimetry.
03 · ~500 M AGL HDL + HDS · 3D Hazard & Lighting Scan Hazard Detection Lidar scans the landing zone into a real-time 3D DEM; Hazard Detection Software (HDS) scores safe, sunlit ridges within the 500 m divert footprint.
04 · DIVERT → TOUCHDOWN DQG · Dual Quaternion Guidance Adaptive minimum-fuel guidance retargets the vehicle in real time to the optimal candidate spot (modeled above via Best spot within 500 m).
